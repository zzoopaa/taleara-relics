Halloween 2026 Cosmetics & Items

The 2026 Halloween event is Taleara's first seasonal event. It was released on October 20th, 2026 after the BETA-11 update. It came with 26 new items in the following categories:

  • 18 ghost themed cosmetics
  • 3 weapon reskins
  • 3 generic Halloween cosmetics
  • 3 miscellaneous items

The 18 ghost themed cosmetics are from the Goodie Bag (2026), pictured above. The three weapon reskins all have unique particle effects and sounds. The 3 generic Halloween cosmetics are crafted directly and are all pumpkin heads. The 3 miscellaneous items have various purposes and can be found in the 'Other' section at the bottom of this page.

The Halloween 2026 items can be obtained during the event by harvesting Halloween Flux through fishing, killing mobs, or completing puzzles. The flux can then be crafted into various items at the crafting station on Lady Hill in the Eastern Swamp. The Halloween 2026 cosmetics are not truly limited, as they will return every year for Halloween. The cost of items from older years will increase over time, and older Goodie Bags will cost more flux.
