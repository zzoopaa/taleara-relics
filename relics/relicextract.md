### Appearance & Effects

In-game description: The colors of the relic present. Recolorable items can be colored to the "Super Relic" color variant at any dyer.

Relic Extract is not a relic, but is a related item that can be crafted from Relic Presents. The crafting recipe for Relic Extract is unlocked when the player gets a Relic Present for the first time. A player can craft 1 Relic Present into 2 Relic Extract. Relic Extract can be applied to any recolorable non-relic rarity gear or accessory to give it the <span class="recolorable">Super Relic</span> paint.

<br>

### Inspiration

Tid wanted to give players the ability to give normal accessories and any other piece of recolorable gear the Super Relic paint.
