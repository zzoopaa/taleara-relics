### Appearance & Effects

In-game description: spacebubble

Oof's Space Head is a galaxy that replaces the player's head. It has a central cloud that is made up of deep blacks and purples, with a ring of brighter purples and whites around the outside. Oof's Space Head is recolorable and has one unique color, Space Purple.

<br>

### Inspiration

Oof's Space Head appears to be inspired by a black hole. The center looks like the event horizon of a black hole and the ring is very similar to the accretion disk of material that often appears around black holes. Oof's Space Head used to be named Oof's Space Halo. Oof's Space Head used to not be recolorable, but this was changed in August 2026.
