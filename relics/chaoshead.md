### Appearance & Effects

In-game description: Defining feature of the lord of the frenzied flame

The Chaos Head is a ball of black and red fire that replaces the player's head. When recolored, the outer corona of the Chaos Head will be recolored while the inner black region will remain unchanged. The Chaos Head is recolorable and has one unique color, Chaos Orange.

<br>

### Inspiration

The Chaos Head was inspired by the head of the [Lord of Frenzied Flame](https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Frenzied_Flame) from Elden Ring. The Chaos Head's owner, SIMIBUS, likes Elden Ring and chose the Chaos Head for this reason. The Chaos Head used to not be recolorable, but this was changed in August 2026.
