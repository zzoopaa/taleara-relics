### Appearance & Effects

In-game description: No one will know who you are now

The Paper Mask was a relic that was removed from the upon request from its original owner, Solar. It was replaced with [Solar's System](./solarssystem.html). The Paper Mask looked like a piece of lined notebook paper with a simple smiley face drawn on top, which would cover the player's face.

<br>

### Inspiration

The owner, Solar, was doing a little trolling with this 'relic'.
