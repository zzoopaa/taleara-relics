### SUPER Relics???

Super relics are exceedingly rare variants of normal relics. They can only be obtained by opening relic presents, and have a 1/25 chance to be dropped. All relics, even ones that cannot be recolored, have a super relic variant. The recolorable part of a super relic will have a changing rainbow gradient instead of a static color, similar to a piece of equipment dyed with [Relic Extract](./relicextract).

<br>

### History

The firt super relic to ever be unboxed was the super relic [Veno Snake](./venosnake), and was opened by the player Zoopa (Spielmitmir22) [That's me!]. The first seven known super relics to be unboxed, in order, are:

1. [Veno Snake](./venosnake)

3. [Celestial Champion Chestplate](./celestialchampionset)

5. [Ace's Deck](./acesdeck)
6. [Kaylie's Halo](./kaylieshalo)
7. [Bojji](./bojji)
