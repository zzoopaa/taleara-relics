Appearance & Effects

In-game description: You got a name?

Luxe Body Ink are a set of markings that appears on the player's body. They are geometrical stripes that cover the player's skin. Luxe Body Ink has one unique color, Luxe Gold, and can be recolored.


Inspiration

Luxe Body Ink was inspired by the character Chamber from Valorant, who has similar gold markings on his body.
