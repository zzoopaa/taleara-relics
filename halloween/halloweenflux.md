### Appearance & Effects

In-game description: The main halloween event resource. Use it to craft Goodie Bags which contain time-gated cosmetics.

Halloween Flux can be obtained by fishing, killing mobs, or completing puzzles during the event. A player will always get 2 Halloween Flux at a time. It can be used for various recipes at the event crafting statio on Lady Hill in the Eastern Swamp.
