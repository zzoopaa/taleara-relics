### Appearance & Effects

In-game description: A tin locket. Instead of holding a picture, it holds a ghost pretending to be one. A truly limited badge for attending the 2026 Halloween event.

The Ghost in a Locket is the only truly exclusive item from Taleara's Halloween 2026 event. All other items except for this one can be obtained in future events. It is not a cosmetic, and has no other uses. Player's were able to obtain the Ghost in a Locket by opening at least one Goodie Bag during the 2026 Halloween event.
