Secrets in Taleara

This page is not related to relics in any way, I just wanted to add a page for secrets.

The developer, Tid, has added quite a few secrets to the game, some of which can be viewed or accessed by players and some of which cannot.


Full List

Super Secret Settings

The Super Secret Settings are 'fun' settings only intended for messing around in the game. To enable them, player's can type 'supersecretsettings' in chat, or click the tiny brown square in the top left of the settings menu.

1 Petabyte Zip Bomb

The 1 Petabyte Zip Bomb is a secret item that only developers can spawn into player's inventories. The item itself weighs a hundred billion pounds or some ridiculous number like that. This means it cannot be stored and will permanently hinder the player's movement speed if they attempt to keep it. It also cannot be sold for money. The only way to get rid of it is to drop it on the ground.

Rabbit Hole

The Rabbit Hole is a clone of the Rabbit Hole from Fantastic Frontier. It is only able to be accessed by players if an admin teleports them there. It spawns a model of The Parasite at night, which does not do anything. There is even a cage for Evil Zoopa.

Judgement Island

Judgement Island is a clone of the island of the same name from Arcane Adventures. This is the largest secret in Taleara, and it even has an obelisk at which players can set their spawn. There are grappling spots, lava pits, a PVP arena in the middle, a pirate ship, and more little secrets on the island. Players can only be teleported to Judgement Island by admins, but can set their spawn there to go anytime they want. The ocean around Judgement Island has the same fish as the ocean at the beaches of the mainland.

Gorglin Baddie Plaque

At the bottom of the bottomless pit cave lies a plaque which denotes that Acestar10 and YourAverageFrenchCat were promised a Gorglin Baddie if they could reach the bottom of the pit. They did, but they did not receive their reward.
